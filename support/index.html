HERE FOR YOUR DAY

A little help
for the rhythm you want.

Whether you’re adding a water break, a mindful pause, a meal, or a movement routine, these steps can help FlowWell fit into your day.

IN THIS GUIDEStart with one small momentPlanning routinesRemindersPersonalitiesFloating notchTroubleshooting
01

Start with one small moment

Open FlowWell from its Chrome toolbar icon, choose a personality, and add one routine you want to remember. It could be a glass of water, a meal break, a supplement, movement, or meditation. You can add more whenever you’re ready.

  1. Choose Friendly, Gamified, or Workout mode.
  2. Choose a routine, then set the days and time that work for you.
  3. Allow browser notifications if you want Chrome notifications for due reminders.
02

Make room for the routines you choose

In the planner, choose a day, add a routine, then set a time and repeat days. FlowWell supports meditation, movement (called Exercise in Workout mode), hydration, nutrition, supplements, focus, and custom routines. For a medication reminder, use a custom routine and set a schedule you already follow; FlowWell does not choose medication or dosage.

03

Let reminders meet you in the day

FlowWell uses Chrome alarms to schedule reminders and Chrome notifications for alerts. Allow notifications if you want system alerts when a reminder is due. You can also check your planner or the floating notch to see what you planned.

Browser and operating-system behavior can affect reminder timing. If something does not appear when expected, open FlowWell to check whether it is scheduled, due, or completed.

04

Choose a tone that feels like yours

Change the mode in planner settings. Friendly brings warm greens and gentle language; Gamified uses purple and light progress cues; Workout uses dark surfaces, coral accents, and exercise wording for movement reminders.

05

Keep the next moment in view

The floating notch can show your next reminder or active focus-session progress while you browse. Open it to see the details. You can finish or skip a due reminder, and pause, resume, or finish a focus session.

You can change notch visibility and position in settings, and add domains where it should stay hidden. The notch does not appear on Chrome extension pages.

06

Troubleshooting

I cannot see the notch＋

Check that notch visibility is enabled in FlowWell settings, that the current site is not on your hidden domains list, and refresh the page. The notch only appears on regular HTTP and HTTPS web pages.

A reminder did not show up＋

Check that the routine is enabled for the current day and time. Allow Chrome notifications if you expect a system notification. You can also open FlowWell to review your planner and due moments.

The notch is not changing＋

Refresh the web page. If the extension was just updated, reopening the page can reconnect the notch to FlowWell.

How do I reset my data?＋

FlowWell settings include a delete-all-data action. Deletion removes FlowWell planner records stored in the browser.
