A FEW THOUGHTFUL ANSWERS

Good to know.

Visit Support ↗
How does FlowWell support my day?＋

FlowWell helps you plan recurring routines, make room for focus, and see the moments you complete. Gentle reminders in the floating notch can bring your next planned moment back into view while you browse. You choose what to schedule and when.

What does the floating notch do?＋

It keeps your next reminder or active focus session visible. Open it to see details, finish or skip a due reminder, pause or finish focus, and return to your planner.

Can I change the personality?＋

Yes. Choose Friendly, Gamified, or Workout mode in FlowWell settings. Each has its own colors and tone. Workout mode calls movement reminders “Exercise.”

Can FlowWell tell me what to eat or take?＋

No. You choose the routines and timing; FlowWell does not give health, nutrition, or medication advice. Supplements have a reminder category. For medication, you can create a custom reminder for a schedule you already follow—FlowWell does not choose a medicine or dosage.

Where can I find privacy and setup help?＋

See the privacy overview for what the extension code shows about storage and permissions, and Support for setup and troubleshooting.
