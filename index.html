A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good to know.

Visit Support ↗
What is FlowWell?＋

FlowWell is a day planner and wellness companion for Chrome. It helps you plan recurring routines, start focus sessions, and see the moments you complete each day.

What does the floating notch do?＋

It shows an upcoming or due reminder, or the progress of an active focus session. Open it to see details, complete or snooze a reminder, pause or finish focus, and open your planner.

Can I change the personality?＋

Yes. Choose Friendly, Gamified, or Workout mode in FlowWell settings. Each has its own colors and tone. Workout mode calls movement reminders “Exercise.”

Does FlowWell give health advice?＋

No. FlowWell lets you schedule reminders and routines that you choose. It does not assess your health or recommend supplements, exercise, or nutrition.

Where can I find privacy and support details?＋

See the privacy overview for what the extension code shows about storage and permissions, and Support for setup and troubleshooting.
