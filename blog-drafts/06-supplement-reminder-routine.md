# Create a Supplement Reminder That Fits Your Existing Routine

If you already take a supplement according to guidance you trust, remembering the routine may be the hardest part of fitting it into a busy day. A simple reminder can make the time visible. It should not choose a supplement for you, tell you whether you need one, or recommend an amount.

## Begin with your own plan

Use the instructions on the product or the guidance you have received from a qualified professional. If you have questions about whether a supplement is appropriate for you, ask a clinician or pharmacist. A productivity extension cannot assess your health, nutrition needs, or possible interactions.

When the schedule is clear, pick a time that already makes sense in your day. You might choose a point after breakfast, before leaving work, or another cue that fits your existing routine. The best reminder time is one that matches the instructions you follow and is practical for you.

## Schedule a reminder you control

FlowWell includes a supplement reminder category in its planner. You choose the days and time, then keep the reminder alongside other routines such as water breaks, meals, movement, or meditation. The floating notch can show a due reminder while you browse, helping bring your planned moment back into view.

If you prefer, use a neutral label that is easy for you to recognize without displaying more detail than you want. On a day when your schedule changes, update the plan to reflect the routine you actually intend to follow. Do not use an app reminder to change product instructions or professional advice.

## Keep the reminder from becoming noise

Start with one reminder rather than adding many alerts at once. If it regularly appears during a meeting or at an inconvenient time, move it to a more workable moment—while staying consistent with the instructions you follow. If the routine no longer applies to you, remove or disable the reminder.

FlowWell lets you finish or skip a due reminder and return to your planner to adjust the schedule. Skipping is not a recommendation about taking or not taking a supplement; it simply records how you handled that reminder in the app. For product-specific decisions, use qualified guidance.

## Review the schedule after a few days

Ask whether the time was easy to notice and whether it fit your real calendar. A reminder that clashes with your workday is unlikely to help. FlowWell’s daily summary can show the moments you marked complete, giving you a record of how your plan was used. It cannot confirm what you took.

The practical value of a supplement reminder is modest but clear: it puts a cue in a place you already look. Keep the health choices yours, set only the schedule you intend to follow, and adjust the timing when your day requires it.
